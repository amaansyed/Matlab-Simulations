# Aerospace Engineering MATLAB Simulation Portfolio

1. **01_projectile_trajectory_drag.m** — 2D ballistic trajectory with quadratic aerodynamic drag, solved with ode45.
2. **02_two_body_orbit_propagation.m** — Numerical propagation of a satellite orbit under two-body gravity, plotted in 3D around Earth.
3. **03_satellite_drag_decay.m** — Simplified orbital altitude decay of a LEO satellite due to atmospheric drag over 30 days.
4. **04_aircraft_longitudinal_dynamics.m** — Linearized state-space model of aircraft short-period and phugoid modes, with step response and pole-zero map.
5. **05_pitch_attitude_pid_control.m** — Closed-loop PID controller tracking a commanded pitch attitude.
6. **06_rocket_ascent_staging.m** — Two-stage rocket vertical ascent simulation with variable mass and thrust.
7. **07_isa_atmosphere_model.m** — International Standard Atmosphere model computing temperature, pressure, and density up to 25 km.
8. **08_airfoil_lift_drag_polar.m** — Thin airfoil theory lift curve with stall modeling and drag polar, including best L/D point.
9. **09_reentry_trajectory_heating.m** — Ballistic atmospheric reentry trajectory with Sutton-Graves stagnation-point heat flux estimation.
10. **10_hohmann_transfer.m** — Delta-v and transfer time calculation for a Hohmann transfer, with orbit visualization.
